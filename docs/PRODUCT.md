# Product

**OSC.ME: Practise. Perform. Perfect.** A personal AI OSCE examiner for physical examinations.

## Problem
Students can rehearse an examination endlessly, but rarely get feedback on what their hands actually did. Qualified observers are scarce, and peer feedback is inconsistent. Existing AI OSCE tools (Geeky Medics, Quesmed, TalkOSCE, Diagnosica and others) rehearse only the spoken part.

## Initial users
UK undergraduate medical students in their clinical years, preparing for finals and the UKMLA CPSA, who already practise in pairs.

## Principles
- Physical examination assessment is the defining innovation; everything else supports it.
- Honest coverage over impressive scores: show what was assessed, what couldn't be, and why.
- Every mark links to timestamped evidence. Seen, not said.
- Formative and educational. It doesn't diagnose, certify competence or replace formal examinations.
- Consent from everyone filmed, including the practice partner. Short retention by default.
- Make peer practice better: the partner can confirm what the camera can't see, and those confirmations are labelled as theirs.

## Moat (to be earned, not claimed)
A validated manoeuvre vocabulary, an evidence-ledger assessment model, consented and expert-marked validation data, setup UX that works with one phone, and published agreement studies.

## Not now
AI patients, history-taking stations, intimate examinations, payments, native apps, custom mark-scheme upload (after the MVP).
