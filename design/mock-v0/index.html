Record your respiratory examination

One phone, one take, about 10 minutes. You get feedback on what your hands did, with timestamps.

Station

Before you press record

  • Prop the phone on the side opposite your dominant hand, at chest height, landscape.
  • Patient and both your hands in frame for the front and the back of the chest.
  • Partner in a vest or sports top. No faces needed above the chin.
  • Say each step if you like. Speech helps us find the moment, but only what we see earns the mark.
